How it works

Three moves. No new meetings.

  1. 01

    Raise it in one field

    Describe what you need. The routing table names the owner, a deputy, and the typical wait - before you hit send.

  2. 02

    It lands with a clock

    The owner sees an inbox sorted by age. One action each: yes, no and why, hand over to a name, or ask one question.

  3. 03

    Waiting becomes visible

    Management sees the wait ledger: median time to first answer, share within the 14-day promise, and where the waiting goes.
